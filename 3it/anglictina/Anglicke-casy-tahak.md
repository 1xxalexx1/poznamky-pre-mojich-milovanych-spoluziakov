# Anglické časy — ťahák na test

Nie je to telepatia. Ten „pocit pre čas“ už máš; pravidlá sú len štítky na veci, ktoré mozog doteraz hádal. ✅

> **Najprv sa pýtaj:** Je to zvyk, deje sa to teraz, je dôležitý výsledok, alebo hovorím o minulosti/budúcnosti?

## Rýchla mapa

| Čas | Základná myšlienka | Príklad |
|---|---|---|
| **Present simple** | zvyk, fakt, rozvrh | I **play** football every Friday. |
| **Present continuous** | deje sa teraz / dočasne | I **am playing** football now. |
| **Present perfect** | minulosť s výsledkom teraz | I **have played** football. |
| **Past simple** | hotová minulosť | I **played** football yesterday. |
| **Past continuous** | dej prebiehal v minulosti | I **was playing** at 5 p.m. |
| **Past perfect** | niečo sa stalo *pred* inou minulosťou | I **had played** before dinner. |
| **will** | rozhodnutie/predpoveď/názor | I **will play** tomorrow. |
| **be going to** | plán alebo viditeľný dôkaz | I **am going to play** tomorrow. |

---

# 1. Present simple

## Kedy ho používam

- **zvyky a rutiny:** things you do repeatedly
- **fakty a všeobecné pravdy:** things that are normally true
- **rozvrhy a pevné programy:** train, school, film, timetable
- **stavy:** like, love, know, want, need, understand, believe

> I get up at seven.  
> Water boils at 100 °C.  
> The lesson starts at eight.  
> I like this song.

## Tvorba

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I/You/We/They **play**. · He/She/It **plays**. |
| zápor | I/You/We/They **do not (don't) play**. · He/She/It **does not (doesn't) play**. |
| otázka | **Do** I/you/we/they play? · **Does** he/she/it play? |

### On, ona, ono = koncovka **-s**

Pri **he / she / it** pridaj k slovesu **-s**:

- play → plays
- read → reads
- work → works

Pravidlá písania, pretože angličtina sa zjavne rozhodla mať aj toto:

- `-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o` → **-es**: watch → watches, go → goes
- spoluhláska + `y` → `-ies`: study → studies
- samohláska + `y` → len `-s`: play → plays
- have → **has**

**Dôležité:** po **doesn't** a **does** je sloveso v základnom tvare.

- ✅ She **doesn't like** coffee.
- ❌ She **doesn't likes** coffee.

## Signálne slová

**always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week, on Mondays**

# 2. Present continuous

## Kedy ho používam

- niečo sa deje **práve teraz**
- dočasná situácia okolo prítomnosti
- zmena alebo vývoj
- dohodnutý budúci plán (často s časom)

> I am doing my homework now.  
> She is staying with her aunt this week.  
> The weather is getting colder.  
> We are meeting Tom at six.

## Tvorba

**am / is / are + sloveso-ing**

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I **am working**. · You/We/They **are working**. · He/She/It **is working**. |
| zápor | I **am not working**. · He **isn't working**. · They **aren't working**. |
| otázka | **Am** I working? · **Is** he working? · **Are** they working? |

### Ako vzniká -ing

- work → working
- make → making (zmizne koncové `e`)
- run → running (krátka samohláska + spoluhláska: zdvojíme)
- lie → lying

## Signálne slová

**now, right now, at the moment, currently, look!, listen!**

## Present simple vs continuous

- I **play** games every evening. → zvyk
- I **am playing** a game now. → práve teraz

### Pozor na stavové slovesá

Tieto sa zvyčajne nedávajú do continuous: **like, love, hate, know, understand, want, need, believe, remember, belong**.

- ✅ I **know** the answer.
- ❌ I **am knowing** the answer.

# 3. Present perfect

## Kedy ho používam

Minulosť, ale **dôležitá pre prítomnosť**. Čas buď nepovieme, alebo ešte neskončil.

- skúsenosť: *Have you ever been to London?*
- výsledok teraz: *I have lost my keys.* (stále ich nemám)
- niečo začalo v minulosti a trvá doteraz: *I have lived here for three years.*
- čerstvá udalosť: *She has just arrived.*

## Tvorba

**have / has + 3. tvar slovesa (past participle)**

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I/You/We/They **have finished**. · He/She/It **has finished**. |
| zápor | I **haven't finished**. · She **hasn't finished**. |
| otázka | **Have** you finished? · **Has** she finished? |

Pravidelné sloveso: `work → worked → worked`  
Nepravidelné sloveso: `go → went → gone`, `see → saw → seen`, `do → did → done`, `eat → ate → eaten`.

## Signálne slová

**ever, never, already, yet, just, recently, lately, for, since**

- **already** = už (väčšinou kladná veta): *I have already eaten.*
- **yet** = už/ešte (otázka alebo zápor, na konci): *Have you finished yet? I haven't finished yet.*
- **just** = práve: *They have just left.*
- **for** = počas dĺžky: *for two hours*
- **since** = od začiatku: *since Monday / since 2024*

## Present perfect vs past simple

Toto je ten hlavný bordel:

- **Past simple**: viem **kedy** sa to stalo a čas je skončený.  
  *I saw that film **yesterday**.*
- **Present perfect**: čas nepoviem / výsledok je teraz.  
  *I have seen that film.*

Nepoužívaj present perfect s hotovým časom: **yesterday, last week, in 2022, ago**.

- ✅ I visited Prague last year.
- ❌ I have visited Prague last year.

# 4. Past simple

## Kedy ho používam

- hotová udalosť v minulosti
- postup udalostí v príbehu
- minulý zvyk alebo stav

> We watched a film yesterday.  
> He opened the door, walked in and sat down.  
> I lived in Zvolen when I was a child.

## Tvorba

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I/You/He/She/It/We/They **worked / went**. |
| zápor | **did not (didn't) + základný tvar**: I didn't work / go. |
| otázka | **Did + podmet + základný tvar?** Did you work / go? |

Pravidelné slovesá majú `-ed`: play → played.  
Nepravidelné sa treba naučiť: go → went, have → had, see → saw, get → got.

**Po did/didn't nikdy nedávaj minulý tvar.**

- ✅ Did she **go** home?
- ❌ Did she **went** home?

## Signálne slová

**yesterday, last week/year, ago, in 2020, when I was...**

---

# 5. Past continuous

## Kedy ho používam

- dej prebiehal v konkrétnom momente v minulosti
- dlhší dej, ktorý prerušila krátka udalosť
- dve veci prebiehali naraz

> At 8 p.m. yesterday, I was studying.  
> I was walking home when it started to rain.  
> While I was cooking, my brother was watching TV.

## Tvorba

**was / were + sloveso-ing**

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I/He/She/It **was working**. · You/We/They **were working**. |
| zápor | I **wasn't working**. · They **weren't working**. |
| otázka | **Was** he working? · **Were** they working? |

## Past simple vs past continuous

- **past continuous** = dlhší dej na pozadí
- **past simple** = krátka/prerušujúca udalosť

> I **was doing** my homework when Mum **called**.

Často:

- **when** + krátka udalosť: *I was sleeping **when** the phone rang.*
- **while** + prebiehajúci dej: ***While** I was sleeping, the phone rang.*

---

# 6. Past perfect

## Kedy ho používam

Keď sú v minulosti **dve udalosti** a chceš ukázať, ktorá bola prvá.

> When we arrived, the film **had started**.  
> She was tired because she **had not slept** well.

Najprv sa začal film, až potom sme prišli. Všetko je síce v minulosti, ale past perfect ide na **staršiu minulosť**. Dinosaurí minulý čas, skrátka.

## Tvorba

**had + 3. tvar slovesa** pre všetky osoby.

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I/She/They **had finished**. |
| zápor | I/She/They **hadn't finished**. |
| otázka | **Had** you/she/they finished? |

> After I **had eaten**, I went to bed.

**Nedávaj ho všade len preto, že môžeš.** Ak ide o obyčajný minulý príbeh bez potreby poradia, past simple stačí.

---

# 7. Budúcnosť: will

## Kedy ho používam

- rozhodnutie urobené **teraz**, v momente rozprávania
- sľub, ponuka, hrozba
- predpoveď založená na názore

> The phone is ringing — I **will answer** it.  
> I **will help** you.  
> I think it **will rain** tomorrow.

## Tvorba

**will + základný tvar slovesa**

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I **will call**. / I'll call. |
| zápor | I **will not call**. / I won't call. |
| otázka | **Will** you call? |

Po *will* nikdy nedávaj `to`, `-s`, `-ed` ani `-ing`.

- ✅ She will come.
- ❌ She will to come.

## Signálne slová

**I think, probably, maybe, I hope, I believe**

---

# 8. Budúcnosť: be going to

## Kedy ho používam

- plán alebo úmysel, ktorý existoval **pred chvíľou rozprávania**
- predpoveď založená na dôkaze, ktorý vidím teraz

> I **am going to study** tonight. (už som sa rozhodol)  
> Look at those clouds. It **is going to rain**. (dôkaz)

## Tvorba

**am / is / are + going to + základný tvar slovesa**

| | Tvar |
|---|---|
| kladná veta | I **am going to work**. · She **is going to work**. · They **are going to work**. |
| zápor | I **am not going to work**. · He **isn't going to work**. |
| otázka | **Are** you going to work? · **Is** she going to work? |

## will vs going to

- *I'll make dinner.* → práve som sa rozhodol.
- *I'm going to make dinner.* → plánoval som to už predtým.
- *I think Slovakia will win.* → môj názor/predpoveď.
- *They are going to win — they're 4–0 up.* → viditeľný dôkaz.

# Mini rozhodovací strom na teste

1. **Zvyk, fakt, rozvrh?** → present simple
2. **Deje sa to teraz/dočasne?** → present continuous
3. **Minulosť, ale dôležitý je výsledok teraz; neviem/nepoviem kedy?** → present perfect
4. **Skončená minulosť s `yesterday`, `last`, `ago`, rokom...?** → past simple
5. **Prebiehalo to v minulosti, často keď sa stalo niečo iné?** → past continuous
6. **Jedna minulá udalosť sa stala ešte pred druhou?** → past perfect pre tú prvú
7. **Rozhodnutie teraz / sľub / názor?** → will
8. **Starší plán alebo dôkaz pred očami?** → going to

# Najčastejšie pasce

- ❌ *He don't play.* → ✅ *He doesn't play.*
- ❌ *She is play.* → ✅ *She is playing.*
- ❌ *Did you went?* → ✅ *Did you go?*
- ❌ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✅ *I saw him yesterday.*
- ❌ *I am go to study.* → ✅ *I am going to study.*
- ❌ *She will to call.* → ✅ *She will call.*
- ❌ *I have been there last year.* → ✅ *I went there last year.*

# Formy, ktoré sa fakt oplatí vedieť

| Infinitive | Past simple | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| be | was/were | been |
| go | went | gone |
| do | did | done |
| have | had | had |
| see | saw | seen |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| get | got | got |
| make | made | made |
| take | took | taken |
| write | wrote | written |
| speak | spoke | spoken |
| come | came | come |
| give | gave | given |
| know | knew | known |
| find | found | found |

> **Na poslednú chvíľu:** Najviac bodov ti pravdepodobne zje rozdiel medzi **past simple × present perfect**, **present simple × continuous** a **will × going to**. Prebehni si tie porovnania dvakrát namiesto toho, aby si pozeral na všetkých osem časov ako na jednu veľkú gramatickú polievku.
